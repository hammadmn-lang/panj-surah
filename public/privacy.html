🛡️ Privacy Policy Overview

Welcome to Panj Surah: Daily Prayers & Islamic Companion ("Panj Surah", "the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We hold your privacy and trust in the highest regard. As an Islamic worship companion app designed to facilitate daily prayers (Salah), Quranic recitation, Dhikr, Duas, and spiritual growth, our core commitment is to safeguard your personal information and provide a transparent, respectful, and legally compliant experience.

Our Sacred Privacy Guarantee: Panj Surah does NOT track your real-world movements, does NOT sell your personal data to data brokers, and does NOT transmit your location to remote tracking databases. All prayer calculations and Dhikr counts operate locally on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Application accesses, how advertising services (such as Google AdMob) operate within the app, and how your rights are respected across global standards including GDPR, CCPA, and family protection guidelines.

📍 Device Permissions & Information Usage

1. Location Data (GPS & City Selection)

Purpose: To calculate exact daily Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) according to astronomical sun calculations, your chosen Islamic juristic school (Hanafi / Shafi'i), and to calculate the precise heading towards the Kaaba in Makkah (Qibla Compass).

  • On-Device Computation: Your geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) are processed locally inside your device using standard spherical trigonometry.
  • No Tracking: We never log your location trajectory, geofence you, or send your coordinates to marketing partners.
  • Manual Option: If you prefer not to grant GPS permissions, you can manually select your city (e.g. Lahore, London, New York, Istanbul) from our built-in offline database.

2. Device Compass / Magnetometer Sensor

Purpose: Used strictly when you open the Qibla Compass screen to determine the orientation of your mobile device relative to Magnetic North and True North. Sensor data is processed in real time and is never recorded or transmitted.

3. Audio Playback & Notifications

Purpose: Used to play the Adhan call to prayer, 15-minute prayer reminders, and Holy Quran verse recitations by Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy.

  • The app NEVER accesses your microphone or records any ambient audio.
  • Audio playback is purely output-based and user-initiated or triggered by prayer schedule alarms.

4. Offline Storage & Caches

Purpose: Panj Surah utilizes local device storage (IndexedDB, LocalStorage, and CacheStorage) to store:

  • Your completed prayer tallies and 7-day consistency records.
  • Digital Tasbeeh dhikr counts and customized zikr lists.
  • Durood Sharif counters and Manzil reading markers.
  • Downloaded Quran audio files so you can listen offline without mobile data or Wi-Fi.

☁️ User Account, Cloud Backup & Data Deletion Policy

By default, all your prayer progress, Tasbeeh tallies, and calculation settings operate 100% offline directly on your mobile device.

1. User Account Registration & Password Authentication

If you voluntarily choose to enable the User Account & Cloud Backup feature:

  • Email & Password: You register using your email address and a self-chosen secure password (minimum 6 characters). Passwords protect your account and are encrypted to prevent unauthorized access.
  • Forgot & Reset Password: If you forget your password, you can reset your credentials at any time directly through the in-app password reset feature.
  • Synchronized Data: Only your devotional logs (5-prayer streaks, Ramadan fasting tracker entries, lifetime Tasbeeh tallies, Durood counters, and completed Surah bookmarks) are synchronized to enable data recovery when upgrading or replacing devices.

2. Complete User Account & Data Deletion (Google Play & GDPR Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's App Account and Data Deletion Policy, GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure), and CCPA standards, users have the absolute right to permanently delete their account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Profile → User Account & Data Deletion and click "Delete Account & Data". This immediately purges your credentials, erases all cloud-stored prayer backups, and clears your local device logs.
  • Dedicated Deletion Web Portal: Users can visit https://panj-surah-privacy.netlify.app/delete-account.html to submit an account deletion request.
  • Email Deletion Request: You may also request total deletion of your account and data by emailing our developer directly at hammadmn99@gmail.com with the subject line "Account & Data Deletion Request". Requests are executed within 48 hours without retention.
  • Data Retention Period: We do not retain residual copies of your prayer history or passwords once deletion is confirmed.

3. Exporting Your Personal Data (Data Portability)

You have the right to download a complete copy of your prayer logs, Tasbeeh numbers, and custom dhikr presets in standard JSON format at any time from the Profile dashboard or Data Rights modal.

📢 Advertising & Google AdMob Integration

To support the continuous engineering, cloud maintenance, and free distribution of Panj Surah to Muslims worldwide, this application integrates or will integrate third-party mobile advertising through Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited).

1. Information Collected by Advertising Partners

When ad units (such as banner ads, interstitial prayer transition notices, or rewarded video features) are displayed, Google AdMob and its advertising technology partners may automatically collect and process certain technical device data to serve relevant, high-quality, and policy-compliant ads. This data may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID on Android) or vendor identifier.
  • Technical Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, mobile carrier, and screen dimensions.
  • Network & Connection: IP address (used to infer general coarse region/country for local language ads), connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad impressions served, clicks, and diagnostic performance metrics (to prevent ad fraud and invalid click activity).

2. Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads & Consent Management

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • User Consent (Google UMP / CMP): For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the application presents a Consent Management Platform (CMP) allowing you to consent to or decline personalized advertising, cookies, and mobile identifiers.
  • Non-Personalized Ads (NPA): If you choose not to consent to personalized ads, Google AdMob will serve non-personalized advertisements based strictly on contextual content (e.g. general app category or coarse country level) rather than your past digital behavior.
  • Opt-Out on Android: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time through your phone's native settings under Settings → Google → Ads → Reset/Delete Advertising ID.

3. Sacred Context & Ad Quality Standard

We enforce strict AdMob ad category filters to block ads related to gambling, alcohol, adult content, offensive imagery, or anything contradicting Islamic principles. Ads are never displayed during active Holy Quran recitation or mid-prayer sessions.

👶 Children's Privacy

Panj Surah is designed for Muslims of all ages, including families and children learning daily prayers and Quranic recitation.

  • The app strictly adheres to children's digital privacy standards, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
  • When running for children and family audiences, any advertising requests to Google AdMob are automatically tagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment (TFCD) and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent (TFUA), which disables interest-based behavioral tracking and serves only child-safe contextual advertisements.
