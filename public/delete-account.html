📱 Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the Panj Surah app installed on your Android device, you can delete your account and all cloud/local records instantly:

  1. Open the Panj Surah app on your device.
  2. Tap on the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the "User Account & Data Deletion" card.
  4. Click the red "Delete Account & Data" button.
  5. Confirm the prompt. Your credentials, cloud records, and local worship cache will be wiped immediately.

✉️ Option 2: Request Deletion via Email (Web Request)

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request directly to our developer team:

  • Recipient Email: hammadmn99@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account & Data Deletion Request - Panj Surah
  • Information to Include: The registered email address you used to register or log into the Panj Surah app.

🛡️ What Data Is Deleted?

Upon receiving and processing your deletion request, the following information is permanently removed without retention:

  • Account Authentication: Your email address, encrypted password token, and registration timestamp.
  • Prayer Records: All 5-prayer completion logs and streak history.
  • Fasting Calendar: Ramadan logs, voluntary fasting records, and suhoor/iftar checklists.
  • Tasbeeh & Dhikr: Total lifetime counter, daily logs, and custom dhikr presets.
  • Bookmarks & Settings: Surah reading progress, favorite Duas, and notification choices.
Data Retention Period: We do NOT retain residual copies of your prayer logs or credentials. Deletion is irrevocable and completed within 48 hours of your email request.
